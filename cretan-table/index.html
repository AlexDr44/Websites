Our Story

A Taste of Home

Cretan Table was created with one simple idea: to bring the authentic flavours and traditions of Crete to the table.

Our recipes are inspired by generations of Cretan cooking, using fresh local ingredients, traditional techniques and plenty of love.

Whether you're visiting Chania or simply looking for a taste of Greece, we welcome you like family.
